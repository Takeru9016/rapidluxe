# RapidLuxe 2.0 — SEO & Technical Page Requirements

**Priority:** P1

## Requirements

- Unique title/description.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph.
- Relevant structured data.
- Optimized images.
- Avoid unnecessary client components.
- Stable loading/error states.
- Semantic headings, keyboard navigation and accessible labels.
- Meaningful analytics for discovery, enquiry, quote, payment and conversion.

## Review

**P1.** Existing sitemap, robots, Open Graph, PostHog and Sentry infrastructure provides the base. Audit coverage and correctness rather than adding duplicate tooling.
